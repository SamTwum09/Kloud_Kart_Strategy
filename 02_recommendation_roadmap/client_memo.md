## What I found (The major problem i discovered during investigation)
Your biggest problem is not the lack of expensive data platform. 

Kloud Kart's current data arrangements create three immediate business concerns:

Reporting activity competes with the live MySQL database and has already slowed the website.

Operations and Finance produce conflicting Monday figures without a demonstrated shared revenue definition.

Marketing customer-data copies are not adequately tracked.

These issues create operational, reporting and customer-data accountability risks as the business grows.

1.Protect the live website from reporting workloads.

2.Establish a tested and reconciled Monday revenue report.

3.Introduce a documented customer-data accountability process.
