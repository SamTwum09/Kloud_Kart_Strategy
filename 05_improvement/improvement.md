# Improvement beyond the brief: Monday Close Control Workbook

## The problem it solves
Nana currently rebuilds Monday numbers manually while Finance calculates them separately. The company has no shared place to record the metric definition, source totals, exceptions, sign-off or customer export expiry. This creates repeated argument and weak evidence.

## What I built
A reusable Excel workbook with four tabs: (1) `Metric Definition` to agree the revenue rule and owner; (2) `Monday Reconciliation` to record source and report totals, calculate variance and flag mismatches; (3) `Export Register` to record purpose, owner, recipients, approval and expiry; and (4) `Deletion Log` to track systems checked, action, exception and evidence reference. It contains no real customer data and is a control template, not a live integration.

## What it is worth to Nana
It gives Nana and Finance one review surface, makes disagreements visible instead of hiding them in two spreadsheets, and gives the company a starting evidence trail for exports and deletion requests. It should reduce repeated manual explanation; actual time saved must be measured after adoption.

## What it cost
A small workbook build; no software licence or customer data. The real cost is staff discipline: Finance and Operations must sign the definition, and every approved export/deletion action must be logged. The workbook is not a substitute for legal advice, access controls, or automated deletion.
